# Almtal Modernist Alpine Painting

This is the canonical framed wall art painting hanging on the pine timber wall in Dr. Georg Hackenberg's home office in Grünau im Almtal, Upper Austria.

## Visual Design
- **Subject**: Modernist abstract composition featuring stylized alpine forest peaks, architecture, and flowing river curves.
- **Palette**: Deep forest green, earthy terracotta rust, warm ochre, and muted sky teal.
- **Framing**: Slim natural pine wood frame with an off-white passe-partout mat board, delineated with crisp comic ink outlines.
