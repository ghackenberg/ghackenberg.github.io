# Dr. Georg Hackenberg

Dr. Georg Hackenberg is the central protagonist and author of the website. Whenever a preview image, hero illustration, or conceptual comic scene requires the content creator, professor, or software engineer, **Georg must be used as the character**.

## Visual Consistency Rules
1. **Facial Features**: Always preserve the distinct trimmed beard, styled brown hair with temple silver accents, and warm, genuine smile based on `portrait.png`.
2. **Attire Contexts**:
   - **Academic / Consulting / Enterprise**: Dark navy or charcoal suit jacket, collared shirt.
   - **Home Office / Video Call / Everyday**: Friendly, approachable knit sweater or casual collared shirt in warm tones (petrol blue, royal blue, heather grey). Avoid black/dark hoodies in home office scenes.
   - **Software Engineering / R&D / Hands-on Lab**: Dark navy or slate tech pullover, dark t-shirt, or workshop layer.
   - **Outdoor / Recreation / Sustainability**: Sporty casual techwear, outdoor jacket.
3. **Whiteboard & Presentation Staging**:
   - When presenting whiteboard architecture or drawings, Georg is positioned to the side (e.g. left/right third), whiteboard marker in hand, turning toward the viewer, leaving the central whiteboard diagram fully visible and legible.
4. **Style Aesthetic**: Must strictly adhere to the Disney/Pixar comic illustration style with bold black/slate ink outlines, cel shading, and neon brand lighting (`#3b82f6` blue, `#a855f7` purple, `#10b981` green).
