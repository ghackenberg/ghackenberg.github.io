# Visual Archetype: Services (`preview.jpg`)

Service previews represent high-level B2B advisory, enterprise architecture audits, digital transformation roadmaps, and technical leadership workshops.

## Visual Archetype: Executive Architecture Strategy Board

An authoritative, executive corporate consultation and strategic architecture planning session.

## Avatar & Staging Rules
* **Avatar Inclusion**: **Yes** (Dr. Georg Hackenberg as Executive Consultant & Strategic Advisor).
* **Role**: Authoritative enterprise architect and trusted technical advisor guiding executive stakeholders.
* **Attire**: Tailored dark navy or slate suit jacket over a crisp collared dress shirt.
* **Staging**: Seated confidently at a round consultation table reviewing an architecture brief or standing beside an illuminated system map. Warm, approachable, visionary posture.

## Camera Framing & Intra-Environment Variation

When multiple services share the same physical setting (e.g., `campus-office-wels visitor-table-focus`), avoid static "clone traps" by varying the camera angle, body posture, props, and whiteboard architecture:

### The 3-Angle Rotation Strategy
* **Center Frontal (e.g. Software Engineering)**:
  * *Camera*: Straight-on medium consultation view across the round table.
  * *Posture*: Georg seated centered, hands at laptop, direct confident gaze at viewer.
  * *Table & Props*: Open ultrabook, printed architecture blueprints laid out.
  * *Whiteboard*: Hierarchical lifecycle decision tree with brand blue/cyan nodes.
* **Left Offset (15°–25° Three-Quarter View, e.g. Intelligence Engineering)**:
  * *Camera*: Shifted to the left, angled across the table edge towards Georg and the whiteboard.
  * *Posture*: Leaning slightly forward in an active advisory posture, hands operating a tablet or gesturing.
  * *Table & Props*: Luminous tablet with live domain metrics, coffee mug, compute sizing notes.
  * *Whiteboard*: Radial hub-and-spoke cluster architecture with brand purple/blue nodes.
* **Right Offset (15°–25° Window-Side View, e.g. Content Engineering)**:
  * *Camera*: Shifted to the right near the window, capturing natural daylight illumination.
  * *Posture*: Seated comfortably in consultation chair, leaning back holding an architecture brief in hand.
  * *Table & Props*: Design-token color swatch cards, spiral schema notebook, sleek notebook.
  * *Whiteboard*: Horizontal multi-channel pipeline flowchart with brand amber/cyan nodes.
