# Visual Archetype: Interests (`preview.jpg`)

Interest previews illustrate foundational intellectual domains, research passions, and visionary technical disciplines.

## Visual Archetype: Concrete & Relatable Domain Metaphor

A tangible, highly relatable real-world metaphor embodying the core practice and human value of each intellectual discipline, understandable even for non-technical visitors.

## 4-Column Layout Ergonomics
* **Thumbnail Scaling Factor**: Interest cards are rendered in a 4-column layout (~280–320px wide per card on desktop/laptop).
* **Simplicity Contract**: Exactly **1 dominant, bold, concrete focal subject** centered in the 16:9 frame (occupying ~60% of canvas height).
* **Orthographic Frontal Projection**: Planar, direct frontal/top-down alignment with zero perspective tilt for maximum architectural precision and thumbnail readability.
* **Concrete Domain Metaphors**:
  * **AI & Agent Systems**: Planar top-down view of a neural accelerator processor chip (NPU) with an illuminated neural network etched into silicon (input left, output right).
  * **Computer Simulation**: Discrete-event smart factory material flow with automated sorting, sensor gates, and amber event pulses.
  * **Software Engineering**: Iterative & incremental development loop (Plan-Code-Test-Deploy) with an assembling architecture stack.
  * **User Experience**: Frontal multimodal interface uniting dynamic voice & sound audio waveforms, acoustic frequency dials, and tactile slider controls in emerald green.
* **Zero Clutter**: Strictly no tiny micro-details, no cluttered backgrounds, no unreadable text.

## Avatar & Entity Rules
* **Avatar Inclusion**: **STRICTLY NO Georg avatar**.
* **Human Figures**: Strictly zero human figures (including no hands).

## Environment & Palette
* **Environment**: Clean, uncluttered deep slate-black void (`#030712`).
* **Palette**: Dark slate foundation with soft ambient radial glow in domain brand colors:
  * Software Engineering: Brand Blue (`#3b82f6`) and Cyan
  * Computer Simulation: Brand Yellow / Amber (`#f59e0b`) and Gold
  * AI & Autonomous Agents: Brand Purple (`#a855f7`) and Electric Blue
  * User Experience: Brand Green (`#10b981`) and Mint
* **Format & Aspect Ratio**: `AspectRatio: "16:9"`, saved as `preview.jpg` in `src/content/interests/[interest_id]/preview.jpg`.
