# Visual Archetype: Interests (`preview.jpg`)

Interest previews illustrate foundational intellectual domains, research passions, and visionary technical disciplines.

## Visual Archetype: Conceptual Macro-Cosmos / Domain Realm

An epic, visionary macro-landscape embodying the essence and future trajectory of an entire intellectual discipline.

## Avatar & Entity Rules
* **Avatar Inclusion**: **STRICTLY NO Georg avatar (Domain Realm & Cosmic Scale)**.
* **Human Figures**: Strictly zero human figures.
* **Rationale**: Interest domains represent overarching philosophical and scientific disciplines that transcend individual personas.

## Camera Framing & Vista
* **Perspective**: Expansive panoramic wide-angle vista.
* **Macroscopic Elements**: Vast metaphorical landscapes — glowing neural networks weaving through architectural lattices, crystalline digital twin structures floating above cybernetic terrain, or geometric coordinate planes extending into infinity.
* **Composition**: Full bleed edge-to-edge vista without containment boxes, frames, or borders.

## Environment & Palette
* **Environment**: Cosmic domain realm / high-tech metaphorical landscape.
* **Palette**: Deep space slate foundation (`#030712`) infused with ethereal atmospheric nebula clouds, glowing energy filaments, and luminous brand color accents:
  * AI & Autonomous Agents: Brand Purple (`#a855f7`) and Electric Blue (`#3b82f6`)
  * Cyber-Physical Systems: Emerald Green (`#10b981`) and Amber (`#f59e0b`)
  * Software Engineering: Brand Blue (`#3b82f6`) and Cyan
* **Format & Aspect Ratio**: `AspectRatio: "16:9"`, saved as `preview.jpg` in `src/content/interests/[interest_id]/preview.jpg`.
